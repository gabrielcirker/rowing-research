What this archive covers

Distinct research assets from the original project, extended-paper archive, local manuscript archive, November 2026 revision packages and backup folders, and accessible repository history. The JURPA-format PDF is a public copy with its historical contact email removed; all other archived research files are byte-preserved. Exact duplicates are consolidated by SHA-256; provenance lists the packages or snapshots where each file appeared. Source files are linked individually, so relative paths may need to be restored when reconstructing an old snapshot.

Unrelated third-party papers, personal/team rowing documents, system metadata, caches, and private working notes are not published. An earlier audit mentioned a 147-page manuscript, but its bytes were not located in the checked sources. It is not represented here as recovered.
